# Mech JE Exam Plan

Which government exams are still open by age, the timeline to the end of 2027, and the mechanical syllabus ranked by how often each subject is asked in RRB JE, SSC JE and Kerala PSC AE.

**Open it: https://rohith-s98.github.io/mech-je-exam-plan/**

- **All exams:** every exam checked against the general-category age rule, grouped into five prep tracks.
- **Timeline:** expected notifications from Oct 2026 to the end of 2027.
- **Technical:** nine mechanical subjects ranked by weightage, with sub-topics you can tick off (saved in your browser).
- **General section:** marks for reasoning, maths, general awareness and science by exam.

Dates after 27 Oct 2026 are estimates from last year's cycle; confirm on the official site when each one opens. Sources are listed at the bottom of the page.
